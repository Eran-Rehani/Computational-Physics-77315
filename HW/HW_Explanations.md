## Quick Reference: Algorithms by Assignment

- **HW01:** Recurrence instability (golden ratio), Taylor-series finite differences
- **HW02:** Binary search bracketing, Lagrange basis polynomials, cubic Hermite splines
- **HW03:** Simpson & Gauss quadrature (fixed / adaptive), bisection & secant root-finding, Bohr-Sommerfeld-Wilson action integral
- **HW04:** Newton's method ($J\Delta x = -F$), LU solve, polytropic white dwarf finite differences, damped step control
- **HW05:** RK3/RK4 stages, step-doubling error control, Richardson extrapolation, Magnus force + drag ODE
- **HW06:** Staggered Lagrangian grid, leapfrog predictor-corrector, artificial viscosity, CFL time-step, exact Riemann solver
